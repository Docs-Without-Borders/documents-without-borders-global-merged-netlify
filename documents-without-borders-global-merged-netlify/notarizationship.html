Notarizationship™: belonging across borders.

Discover Notarizationship™, a term coined by founder Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé for professional connection, shared learning and belonging among notaries worldwide.

Coined by the founder

A name for professional connection.

Notarizationship™ is the term coined by Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé, Founder of Documents Without Borders Global, for professional connection, shared learning and belonging among notaries.

It recognizes that notarial traditions, responsibilities and legal authority vary across jurisdictions. The shared purpose is to learn from one another while respecting those differences.

Notarizationship™ is a community concept. It is not a legal status, certification, license or government designation. The ™ symbol is not a claim of federal trademark registration.

What the word represents

Three commitments. One global community.

01

Connection

Make space for respectful dialogue among notaries and institutions working within different legal systems.

02

Shared learning

Advance comparative education and exchange good practices without treating different notarial powers as interchangeable.

03

Belonging

Recognize the role notaries play in safeguarding documents, identity and public confidence in their respective jurisdictions.

Founder attribution

A term with a purpose.

The founder coined “Notarizationship” to give language to a vision of notaries connecting globally through education, cooperation and mutual respect.

The concept helps express the initiative's community philosophy; it does not establish a membership association or confer a credential.
