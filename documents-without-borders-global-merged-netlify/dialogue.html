The founding international dialogue, strategic priorities, listening initiative and development roadmap of Documents Without Borders Global.

Our purpose

A shared conversation.
A world of legal traditions.

Documents Without Borders seeks to convene notarial professionals and interested institutions in a constructive global conversation about identity, authenticity, public trust and cross-border documentation.

Notarial systems differ significantly around the world. Our approach begins with listening, accurate representation and respect for national and local law.

This foundational editorial language has been carried into the unified DWB Global website.

Strategic priorities

Three pillars of cooperation

I.

International Dialogue

Connect perspectives across common-law, civil-law and other notarial frameworks through respectful professional exchange.

II.

Education & Understanding

Support comparative learning about notarial roles, professional standards and jurisdiction-specific practices.

III.

Professional Collaboration

Explore responsible partnerships, conferences and knowledge-sharing opportunities with relevant organizations.

Global listening initiative

Every jurisdiction has a perspective worth hearing.

Our proposed listening initiative will invite professionals to share how notarization operates in their jurisdictions, the challenges they encounter and opportunities for greater cross-border understanding.

Participation opportunities are being developed. No survey or formal membership program is currently open through this website.
