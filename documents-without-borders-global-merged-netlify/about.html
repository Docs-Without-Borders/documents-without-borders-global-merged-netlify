The founder

Contact about the initiative ↗

Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé was first commissioned as a New York notary public in 2002 and founded The Notary Giant Professional Service Corporation in 2019. His work in notarial practice informs the ambition to improve understanding of official cross-border document procedures.

The Documents Without Borders Global initiative builds on the conviction that notaries have meaningful roles in their respective jurisdictions, and that the public benefits when professional knowledge is shared accurately and responsibly.

“Connecting Notaries. Understanding Borders. Building Trust.”

DWB Global is presented as an educational initiative. Its ultimate organizational, legal and governance arrangements are subject to formal confirmation.

Founder-originated language

Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé coined “Notarizationship” to describe the professional connection, shared learning and belonging envisioned through Documents Without Borders Global.

What Notarizationship™ means

Accountability

Ambition deserves transparent governance.

The proposed future framework includes independent oversight, documented content validation, appropriate financial reporting and responsible governance.

Board composition, charitable status, formal chapters, partnerships and government recognition will be announced only if established and verified.
