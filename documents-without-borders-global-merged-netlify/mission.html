Our mission

Building trust through understanding.

An international educational initiative grounded in accuracy, professional respect and the realities of different legal systems.

Our mission

Connect. Educate. Respect every jurisdiction.

To connect notaries worldwide, promote professional recognition and belonging, and provide accessible education on apostilles, authentication and legalisation.

Our vision is a world where individuals, families and businesses can find clear, reliable information about the authorities and procedures relevant to their cross-border documents.

“We do not erase the legal borders between notarial systems. We work to make those borders easier to understand.”

The founding initiative describes its mission as facilitating international dialogue, comparative education and cooperation while recognizing the distinct legal authority of each jurisdiction.

What we stand for

Principles before promises.

01

Legal respect

Notaries have different public functions and legal authority across countries and territories. We do not claim these systems are interchangeable.

02

Evidence and accuracy

Reliable public guidance needs authoritative sources, periodic reviews and clearly labeled uncertainty.

03

Human judgment

Technology, including AI, can assist research and access. Qualified humans remain responsible for legal and professional decisions.

The distinction matters

Education is not authorization.

DWB Global does not issue apostilles, certify notaries, provide legal opinions, or determine whether a receiving authority must accept a document.

Our proposed materials will direct users to official competent authorities and encourage verification of route-specific requirements.
